Frequently asked questions

Is Révise free?
To start, yes: the Découverte plan is free, with no card, and includes one subject, one document and 3 AI generations. Premium opens everything, from €3.33 a month with the yearly plan.
How does the free trial work?
Your card is asked for, but nothing is charged for 3 days. Cancel before the end and you pay nothing. An e-mail reminds you of its end date as soon as it starts. The trial is offered once per account.
How do I cancel?
Settings, Subscription, “Cancel my subscription”: three clicks, no need to write to anyone. Premium stays open until the end of the period already paid, then nothing more is charged.
What if Premium isn’t right for me?
Write to us within 7 days of your first payment: you’ll be refunded, no questions asked.
What is Express?
A single payment of €4.99 for 7 days of Premium on one subject, with no subscription or renewal: for an exam coming up. Twice a semester at most. If you switch to yearly within 14 days, that €4.99 is taken off.
Can I pause my subscription over the summer?
Yes, with the monthly subscription: up to 3 months without payment, once a year. Your data is kept, and you can still cancel at any time.
I pay monthly: do I lose what I paid if I switch to yearly?
No. From your second monthly payment, what you paid over the last 12 months is taken off the yearly price.
Are my courses sent anywhere?
Your courses stay on your device. Only the passages a task needs are sent to the AI (Google’s Gemini), and Révise doesn’t keep them.
Is payment safe?
Payment goes through Stripe, which also handles invoices. Révise never sees your card number.
Do I need to install anything?
No: Révise works in the browser, on computers and phones. You can add it to your home screen like an app.
